The Story Behind Quiet Growth

A journal about growing up — one ordinary day at a time.

Thank you for stopping by. Let me introduce myself, and this journal, in my own words.

1. About Chi

Hi, I’m Chi Nguyen, the person behind Quiet Growth.

I’m living and studying in Finland, far from where I grew up. Living in a new country has a way of turning ordinary days into lessons: learning to cook for myself, getting used to long dark winters, making new friends in a language I’m still learning, and figuring out who I am when everything around me is unfamiliar.

I’m not the most confident speaker, but I’ve always expressed myself more clearly in writing. When something happens that I can’t quite explain — a hard week, a small win, a feeling I don’t understand yet — writing is how I make sense of it.

I don’t think I will ever be completely “grown up.” I grow through experiences, mistakes, and restarts, often without noticing it at the time. That slow, honest kind of growing is what this journal is about.

2. About Quiet Growth

Quiet Growth is my personal journal, written in public. Here I share stories from my everyday life, the lessons I pick up along the way, and the thoughts I keep coming back to: about growing up, living abroad, relationships, habits, and becoming a little kinder to myself.

I started this journal for three simple reasons. Having a place to publish gives me a gentle push to write regularly, and the more I write, the better I get. Writing also helps me untangle my thoughts, especially when life feels complicated. And most of all, I want to keep these moments. A few years from now, I want to read back through these pages and see how much I have changed.

Writing is how I find out what I actually think.

I also built and manage this website myself, from designing the pages to publishing every post. Like everything else here, it’s a work in progress that grows a little with each update.

The name holds what I believe. “Quiet” because the most important changes in life rarely happen loudly. “Growth” because, no matter how slow it feels, every ordinary day is still shaping who we become.

If you are also growing up in your own quiet way, I hope these pages feel like a conversation with a friend.

I don’t have everything figured out, and I don’t need to. I just want to keep growing, slowly and kindly, into the best version of myself.

— Chi, writing from Finland
