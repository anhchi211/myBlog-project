Hi, I’m Chi

I don’t learn best from theory alone. I learn by doing first, then connect them back to theory. I’m not the most confident speaker, but I express myself more clearly through writing.

Quiet Growth is where I document that process — of still learning, still rebuilding, and still becoming.

My story in 6 lines

I started out in hospitality and slowly found my way into business and tech. Along the way I learned that I learn fastest by building projects, not by waiting until I feel ready.

I’m often unsure, so I build to find clarity. To stay consistent, I break things into small steps, and I write in public to keep myself accountable. Where I’m heading: becoming an IT Business Analyst.

What I believe

I believe today can be a little better than yesterday, and that growth doesn’t have to be loud to be real. Small systems change more than big bursts of motivation.

I also believe clarity comes from building, not just thinking — and that you can restart anytime, as many times as you need.
